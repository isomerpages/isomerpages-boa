14 Sep 2026

### **Notice of office relocation and temporary closure**

Please be informed that the Board’s office and service counter will be closed from **15 September 2026 to 18 September 2026** to facilitate the relocation of the Board’s office. <br/>

The Board’s office will reopen at its new premises on **21 September 2026** at the following address: <br/>

**45 Maxwell Rd #03-02 Singapore 069118** <br/>

Please note that all visits to the Board’s office are **strictly by appointment only**. Members of the public and registered persons are required to make an appointment prior to visiting the Board’s office. <br/>

For enquiries, please contact the Board at BOA_Enquiry@boa.gov.sg. <br/>

The Board regrets any inconvenience caused during this period and appreciates your kind understanding and cooperation. <br/>
